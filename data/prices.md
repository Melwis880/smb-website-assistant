# Prices

> DEMO DATA: Brightsmile Dental is a fictional clinic. All prices are invented for the demo.

All prices are in euros (EUR) and include VAT where applicable. Prices are a guide; your dentist
confirms the exact cost in a written treatment plan before any treatment starts.

## Payment
We accept debit card, credit card and bank transfer. Payment is due at the end of each appointment.
For treatments above EUR 1,000 we offer interest-free payment in up to 6 monthly instalments.
