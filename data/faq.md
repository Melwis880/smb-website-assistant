# Frequently asked questions

> DEMO DATA: Brightsmile Dental is a fictional clinic. All answers are invented for the demo.

## Are you accepting new patients?
Yes, we are accepting new patients of all ages. The first visit is a new patient check-up.

## How do I book an appointment?
You can book by phone, by email, or by leaving your details in this chat. The front desk confirms
every appointment by phone or email within one working day.

## Do you take insurance?
We work with most major dental insurers and can bill some of them directly. We bill DemoCare and
SmileSure directly. For other insurers you pay at the clinic and claim the cost back. Bring your
insurance card to your appointment.

## Does the dentist speak English?
Yes, all our dentists speak English. Two of our dentists also speak German and Spanish.

## I am nervous about the dentist. Can you help?
Yes. Tell us when you book and we will plan a longer appointment, explain every step, and agree on a
stop signal. We do not offer sedation or general anaesthesia.

## What should I do in a dental emergency outside opening hours?
Call the regional out-of-hours dental service on 112-DEMO (demo number). If you have severe swelling
that affects breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## How long does a first visit take?
A new patient check-up takes about 60 minutes, including X-rays and a treatment plan discussion.
