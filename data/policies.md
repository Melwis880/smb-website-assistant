# Policies

> DEMO DATA: Brightsmile Dental is a fictional clinic. All policies are invented for the demo.

## Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations and missed
appointments are charged a fee of EUR 30.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule your appointment.

## Children
Patients under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.

## Privacy
We store patient data in line with data protection law. Contact details shared in the website chat
are used only to arrange your appointment and are deleted after 90 days if you do not become a patient.

## Medical advice
The website assistant cannot give medical advice or a diagnosis. For pain or symptoms, please call
the clinic or book an appointment.
