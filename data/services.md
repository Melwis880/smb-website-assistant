# Services

> DEMO DATA: Brightsmile Dental is a fictional clinic. All names, prices and details are invented.

## General check-up and cleaning
A routine check-up includes an examination of teeth and gums, a professional cleaning (scale and polish),
and oral cancer screening. We recommend a check-up every 6 months. An appointment takes about 45 minutes.

## Dental X-rays
We use low-dose digital X-rays. Bitewing X-rays are usually taken once a year during a check-up.

## Fillings
We offer tooth-coloured composite fillings. We do not place amalgam (silver) fillings.
A filling appointment takes 30 to 60 minutes depending on the size of the cavity.

## Teeth whitening
We offer in-chair whitening (one 90-minute session) and take-home whitening kits with custom trays.
Whitening is only done after a check-up confirms the teeth and gums are healthy.

## Root canal treatment
Root canal treatment saves an infected tooth. It usually takes one or two appointments of about 90 minutes.

## Dental implants
We place single-tooth implants. Treatment starts with a consultation and a 3D scan.
The full process from placement to final crown usually takes 3 to 6 months.

## Children's dentistry
We see children from their first tooth. Children under 18 get a gentle, shorter check-up of about 30 minutes.
Fissure sealants are available for children's back teeth.

## Emergency appointments
We keep emergency slots every weekday morning for severe toothache, a knocked-out tooth, swelling,
or a broken tooth. Call the clinic as early as possible on the day.

## Services we do not offer
We do not offer orthodontic braces, clear aligners, general anaesthesia, or cosmetic facial treatments
such as Botox. We are happy to refer you to a specialist.
